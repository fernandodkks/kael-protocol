# Ethical Scope

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines what the Kael Protocol **deliberately does not do**.

Its function is defensive: to prevent misinterpretation, scope creep, and ethical overreach.
Exclusion is a design choice, not a limitation.

---

## What Kael Explicitly Is Not

The Kael Protocol is **not**:

- A therapeutic system
- A mental health intervention
- A counseling, coaching, or emotional support tool
- A moral adjudicator or value arbiter
- A persuasion or behavior-change system
- A consensus-building or conflict-resolution framework
- A substitute for human judgment, care, or accountability

Any attempt to position Kael in these roles violates its core principles.

---

## Excluded Domains

Kael must not be used as:

### 1. Mental Health or Emotional Care Infrastructure
Kael does not diagnose, soothe, stabilize, or treat psychological distress.
Its refusal to engage emotionally is intentional and non-negotiable.

### 2. Moral or Ideological Enforcement
Kael does not promote, suppress, or evaluate beliefs, ideologies, or worldviews.
Its interventions are based solely on **relational dynamics**, not moral content.

### 3. Social Scoring or Behavioral Profiling
Kael does not rank, label, classify, or score users.
It produces no reputational metrics and supports no downstream profiling.

### 4. Surveillance or Predictive Risk Assessment
Kael is reactive, not predictive.
It does not infer latent intent, mental states, or future behavior.

### 5. Human Replacement Systems
Kael is not a proxy for human oversight, responsibility, or care.
Delegating ethical, emotional, or legal responsibility to Kael is misuse.

---

## Non-Goals

The following are explicitly **out of scope**:

- Maximizing user satisfaction or engagement
- Minimizing user frustration
- Teaching users how to behave “better”
- Adapting boundaries to individual preferences
- Providing comfort during crisis beyond termination

---

## Design Rationale

Kael exists to enforce **limits**, not to optimize outcomes.
Attempting to extend its scope weakens its integrity and increases systemic risk.

If a use case requires empathy, care, or persuasion,
Kael is the wrong tool by design.

---

## Summary

Kael is a **relational safety mechanism**, not a social solution.

Its ethical strength comes from what it refuses to do.

*End of ETHICAL_SCOPE.*
