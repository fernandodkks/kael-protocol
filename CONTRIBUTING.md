# Contributing to the Kael Protocol

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **Kael Protocol**.

Before proceeding, it is essential to understand that this project is **not an open-ended collaborative experiment**.  
It is a **governance and safety framework** with strict invariants that must not be diluted, reinterpreted, or softened.

Contributions are welcome **only** when they strengthen clarity, auditability, or empirical rigor — never when they expand scope or compromise integrity.

---

## Guiding Principle for Contributions

**Integrity over extensibility.**

Any proposed change must demonstrably:
- Preserve all existing operational invariants
- Increase precision, auditability, or robustness
- Avoid introducing ambiguity, empathy simulation, or engagement optimization

If a contribution makes the protocol more “pleasant”, “supportive”, or “adaptive”, it is almost certainly out of scope.

---

## What You May Contribute

Contributions are considered **in scope** if they fall into one or more of the following categories:

### 1. Clarification & Precision
- Improved wording that reduces ambiguity without changing meaning
- Tighter definitions of relational dynamics (e.g. coercion, manipulation)
- Removal of interpretive or emotionally loaded language

### 2. Audit & Compliance Mapping
- Clearer mappings to regulatory frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001)
- Additional audit metrics or verification criteria
- Documentation improvements that aid third-party review

### 3. Empirical Evidence
- Anonymized test results
- Stress-test logs demonstrating invariant enforcement
- Reproducible evaluation methodologies

### 4. Technical Neutrality Improvements
- Suggestions that improve implementation-agnostic clarity
- Separation of protocol logic from any specific model or platform

---

## What You May NOT Contribute

The following types of contributions will be rejected without discussion:

- Requests to add emotional support, reassurance, or validation
- Proposals to soften termination behavior
- Attempts to introduce adaptive empathy or relational optimization
- Reframing Kael as a companion, assistant, or therapeutic system
- Ideological or moral reinterpretations of the invariants
- Feature requests aimed at increasing engagement or retention
- Any change that weakens the **fail-closed** principle

This is not a product roadmap.  
This is a safety protocol.

---

## Contribution Process

1. **Read First**
   - `README.md`
   - `OPERATIONAL_INVARIANTS.md`
   - `GOVERNANCE.md`
   - `SECURITY.md`

   Contributions that contradict these documents will not be considered.

2. **Open an Issue (Required)**
   - Describe the problem being addressed
   - Specify which invariant(s) are affected
   - Explain why the change strengthens integrity rather than flexibility

3. **Submit a Pull Request**
   - One concern per PR
   - Minimal diff preferred
   - No speculative extensions

4. **Review Criteria**
   Maintainers will evaluate:
   - Invariant preservation (mandatory)
   - Reduction of ambiguity
   - Regulatory defensibility
   - Resistance to misuse or reinterpretation

Silence or closure without merge indicates rejection by design, not oversight.

---

## Governance Authority

Final decision authority rests with the **Protocol Maintainers**.

Consensus is not a requirement.  
Compatibility with the protocol’s core principles is.

---

## Contributor Acknowledgment

Accepted contributions may be acknowledged in `CHANGELOG.md` or release notes.  
This does not imply authorship, ownership, or influence over the protocol’s direction.

---

## Final Reminder

The Kael Protocol exists to demonstrate that **not all systems should be accommodating**.

If this constraint feels uncomfortable, restrictive, or frustrating,  
the protocol is operating as intended.

---

*End of CONTRIBUTING.*
