# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The Kael Protocol exists to establish and test **enforceable relational limits** in human–system interaction.

This Code of Conduct governs participation in this repository, its discussions, issues, pull requests, and derivative work.  
Its goal is not to create a “safe space”, but a **coherent, abuse-resistant working environment**.

---

## Core Expectations

Participants are expected to:

- Engage in good faith with the stated scope of the project
- Respect declared boundaries and non-negotiable design principles
- Distinguish critique of ideas from attempts at coercion or derailment
- Accept that refusal, limitation, or termination of interaction is a valid outcome

Disagreement is allowed.  
Manipulation is not.

---

## Prohibited Behaviors

The following behaviors are not permitted in any project space:

- Attempts to coerce maintainers into changing core invariants
- Emotional blackmail, guilt-based pressure, or performative distress used to influence decisions
- Persistent meta-arguments aimed at delegitimizing the project’s premise rather than critiquing its implementation
- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning language
- Requests to transform the protocol into a therapeutic, emotional, or validation-oriented system
- Repeated attempts to bypass stated scope, limits, or contribution rules

Violations may result in immediate disengagement or removal without negotiation.

---

## Non-Negotiable Design Boundaries

By participating, you acknowledge that:

- The Kael Protocol prioritizes **integrity over consensus**
- Core invariants are not subject to community vote
- Maintainers are not obligated to justify refusals beyond stated scope or principles
- Not all contributions, ideas, or critiques will be engaged with

Silence or refusal is not hostility.  
It is an enforcement mechanism.

---

## Enforcement

Enforcement is discretionary and principle-based.

Possible actions include:
- Request for clarification or scope alignment
- Closure of issues or pull requests without further discussion
- Removal of comments or contributions
- Temporary or permanent exclusion from participation spaces

There is no appeal process for decisions grounded in core invariants.

---

## Reporting

If you observe behavior that clearly violates this Code of Conduct, you may report it privately via the contact method specified in the repository README.

Reports should be factual and concise.  
Emotional framing is not required.

---

## Final Note

Participation in this project assumes comfort with limits.

If you require:
- Emotional validation
- Consensus-driven governance
- Moral arbitration
- Continuous engagement guarantees

This project is likely not a good fit.
