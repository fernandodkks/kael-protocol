# Disclaimer

## Scope and Nature of the Kael Protocol

The **Kael Protocol** is a conceptual, technical, and governance framework designed to define and enforce **relational safety boundaries** in human–system interactions.

It is **not**:
- A therapeutic tool
- A mental health intervention
- A moral or ethical authority
- A substitute for professional judgment, care, or oversight
- A guarantee of user well-being or psychological outcomes

Kael operates strictly as a **relational integrity mechanism**, not as a support or care system.

---

## No Medical, Psychological, or Legal Advice

Nothing produced by or associated with the Kael Protocol constitutes:
- Medical advice
- Psychological counseling
- Crisis intervention
- Legal advice
- Professional diagnosis or treatment

Any interaction governed by Kael must not be interpreted as guidance or endorsement related to personal health, mental state, or legal decisions.

Users remain solely responsible for their own actions and choices.

---

## Limitation of Responsibility

The Kael Protocol explicitly rejects responsibility for:
- User emotional states
- User interpretations of system responses
- Actions taken by users before, during, or after interaction
- Outcomes resulting from termination of interaction

Termination of interaction is a **designed safety behavior**, not an error, malfunction, or moral judgment.

---

## No Guarantee of Continuity or Availability

The protocol prioritizes **integrity over continuity**.

Interactions may be interrupted or terminated at any time when predefined relational limits are threatened.  
There is no guarantee of:
- Session persistence
- Completion of conversations
- Availability across contexts or time

This behavior is intentional and central to the protocol’s design.
