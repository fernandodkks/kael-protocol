# Kael Protocol — Release Notes

## v0.1.0 — Initial Public Release
**Release Date:** December 2025

### Overview
This is the first public release of the **Kael Protocol**, a relational safety and governance standard for conversational AI systems.

Kael introduces enforceable relational invariants designed to prevent dependency, coercion, and integrity erosion in human–AI interactions.

This release establishes Kael as:
- a safety mechanism, not a product,
- a governance tool, not a UX framework,
- a fail-closed system prioritizing integrity over continuity.

### Included Artifacts
- Core manifesto and ethical framing
- Operational invariants (final refined version)
- Regulatory alignment checklist (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF)
- Governance, scope, and threat modeling documents
- Contribution and conduct boundaries
- Explicit exclusions and misuse prevention notes

### Design Commitments
This release explicitly commits to:

- **Relational Agnosticism**  
  No intervention based on ideology, content, or user intent.

- **Boundary Supremacy**  
  System limits are upheld without negotiation or exception.

- **Non-Judgmental Enforcement**  
  No correction, persuasion, or behavioral guidance.

- **Transparent Termination (Fail-Closed)**  
  Clear, auditable termination when limits are breached.

### Non-Goals (By Design)
Kael does **not** aim to:
- maximize engagement,
- provide emotional support,
- adapt to user preferences,
- resolve human distress.

These exclusions are intentional and documented.

### Stability Notice
Kael v0.1.0 is conceptually stable.
Future releases are expected to:
- refine articulation,
- improve audit clarity,
- incorporate empirical findings,

without weakening or renegotiating core invariants.

### Disclaimer
Use of this protocol does not absolve implementers of legal, ethical, or operational responsibility.
Improper framing or misuse invalidates claims of compliance.
